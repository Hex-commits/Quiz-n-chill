We are creating a combination of a Quiz with a Poker-Game. 

We are dealing a normal Pokergame as a base. 

However, with each flip of a card in the middle, the question will be specified further. First, the category, then the question type (e.g. Flusse in Europa), and then the respective question on the last flip. Everyone, who is still in, should be allowed to answer the question with the given answers from the category simultaneously. 

If they are correct, they win the round. If multiple people are winning, the pot should be split.

Questions will be taken from our question pool, while the options to select the aanswer will be that of a normal quiz round.
